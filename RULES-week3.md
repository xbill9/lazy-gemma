# Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge — Week 3 rules

Source: https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-week3-2026-10-19 (pulled 2026-10-20)

## Theme: "Slow and Steady"
Build something with open-weight models or open-source AI that **rewards laziness**: it does the
work so you don't have to. Tied to International Sloth Day (Oct 20). "Help someone relax, automate
a task they dread, or just make life a little easier." Examples given:
- a local agent that reads your group chats overnight and hands you a two-line summary in the morning
- a wind-down companion that generates a bedtime story or ambient soundscape, entirely offline
- a fridge photo to grocery list tool
- a file and inbox tidier that sorts, renames and archives the clutter you keep putting off

The write-up asks: does running your own model mean it works without internet, keeps your messages
and files on your own machine, or lets you set it up once and never pay again? "Tell us where your
open-based approach worked better than a closed one."

## Hard requirements
- Open-source AI is at the core (open-weight model, open-source agent harness/framework, local
  inference, or a mix). "The open pieces should be what makes your project work."
- The write-up explains why open innovation matters for this build.
- **New project**, started and finished inside the challenge window (opens Oct 19). No PRs to
  existing projects. Commits after the deadline must be noted in the README or the entry may be
  disqualified.
- Published as a DEV post from the template (TEMPLATE-week3.md), tagged `devchallenge`, `hf26challenge`.
- How To Participate: "Tell us what you built and what effort it saves, show us a demo, and explain
  why open innovation matters ... If you can show us the lazy payoff, even better!"
- **Deadline: 2026-10-25, 11:59 PM PDT.** Winners announced the week of Oct 26.
- 18+. Teams of up to 4, one submission, teammates' DEV handles listed in the post.
- One submission per challenge; one win per challenge per submission.
- Non-trivial prior work, including open-source code, must be credited in the post.
- English only for prizes. AI use allowed.
- Optional: DevRelay agent session, embedded with `agent_session` or linked.

## Template change from Week 1
New optional section **"The Lazy Payoff"**: how much time or effort it actually saves, and whether
you (or someone else) used it this week. "What I Built" now asks what effort it saves and who gets to
be lazier.

## Judging (writing is weighted most)
1. Writing quality ("A great project with a thin write-up won't win.")
2. Relevance to the prompt and theme
3. Creativity
4. Technical execution
5. Use of partner technology (optional)
Tie-break: most positive reactions on the DEV post.
